## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛍️ CustomerDNA — Customer Segmentation & Spending Analytics

Applied PCA, K-Means, and Hierarchical Clustering to identify customer segments and analyse spending behaviour. Built a classification model and Power BI dashboards to support targeted marketing decisions.

**Focus:** Customer Analytics · Unsupervised Learning · Power BI

### 🖼️ Synthetic or Real? — AI-Generated Image Detection

Developed a CNN-based image classifier using TensorFlow/Keras, data augmentation, and transfer learning to distinguish AI-generated images from real images.

**Focus:** Deep Learning · Computer Vision · Model Evaluation

### 🌍 Economic Stability in SAARC

Combined macroeconomic indicators, NLP-based sentiment analysis, and time-series forecasting to study economic trends and potential crisis signals across SAARC countries.

**Focus:** Statistical Forecasting · NLP · Economic Analytics · Tableau

### 📉 TailRisk — Health Insurance Claim Severity Modelling

Compared statistical distributions for insurance claim severity and applied tail-risk measures, including Value at Risk (VaR) and Tail Value at Risk (TVaR).

**Focus:** Statistical Modelling · Distribution Fitting · Risk Analytics
